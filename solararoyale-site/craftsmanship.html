Craftsmanship

Built on precision.

Every element, intentionally crafted. Every detail, purposefully placed.

Nothing here is decorative.

Oval-cut centre stone. Prong setting. Accent diamonds. Gallery rails. Band. Every SolaraRoyale piece is engineered from the inside out — measured to the hundredth of a millimetre before a single piece is soldered into place. The exploded view on our homepage isn't a gimmick; it's literally how the ring is built.

01

Design

Every setting starts as a CAD model, tested for stone security before it's ever cast.

02

Casting

Ethically sourced gold, cast in small batches and hand-finished, never mass-poured.

03

Setting

Each stone is hand-set under magnification — prongs shaped to the exact stone, not the reverse.

04

Polishing

A final hand-polish pass, checked against our own quality mark before it ever reaches you.
